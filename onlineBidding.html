ONLINE BIDDING

Buy and Sell cattle — from anywhere.

At Santa Teresa Livestock Auction, you don’t need to be ring side to take part in the action. Our Online Bidding Platform lets you watch the live sale, bid in real time, and manage your purchases securely from your phone, tablet, or computer.

How It Works?

1

Register Online

Create your free account here:

dvauction.com

Once you submit your registration, our team will review and approve your account.

2

Fill our Buyer Registration Form

Click here to download.

Once you complete the form, we will proceed with the registration and conduct the required credit check.

3

Get Ready to Bid

You’ll receive a confirmation with your bidder number and login details. On sale day, just log in and join the live stream.

4

Bid in Real Time

Watch the auction, follow the lots, and place your bids with one click — just like being at the sale barn.

Whether you’re buying from the ranch or on the road, our online platform makes it easy, fast, and reliable to stay connected with every sale.
