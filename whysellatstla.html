WHY SELL AT STLA?

Since 2017, STLA has been the trusted place for producers to sell mexican cattle. We provide strong markets, reliable service, and a seamless selling experience from start to finish, proudly working alongside with the USDA and the Union Ganadera Regional de Chihuahua.

Why sell at STLA?

Fast and Secure Payment

Receive your payment the next business day, directly to your bank account—simple and hassle-free.

Higher Value Recovery

We help you maximize your cattle’s value by reducing shrink losses and ensuring a fair evaluation.

More Buyers, Better Offers

Your cattle gain access to a wide network of serious buyers ready to offer the best price.

Presentation that Highlights Your Cattle

We prepare and classify your cattle to showcase their best qualities, making your lot stand out to buyers.

Full Transparency on Fees and Deductions

All charges are clearly explained beforehand so there are no surprises at the time of final sale.
