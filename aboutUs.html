ABOUT US

Your U.S. Gateway for Mexican Feeder Cattle

Santa Teresa Livestock Auction is the only international livestock auction in the United States — proudly specializing in stocker and feeder cattle from Mexico.

We are a producer-owned company, operating under a concession agreement managed by experienced American livestock operators, ensuring every transaction is handled with professionalism, transparency, and efficiency. We ensure your cattle are in the best hands by providing high-quality fresh feed made with the finest ingredients available in the region. All cattle sold through our auction are located on the U.S. side, having passed rigorous health inspections conducted by both Mexican authorities and the USDA.

Mission

Connect Mexican cattle producers with American buyers through a trusted, efficient, and border-friendly marketplace that strengthens international trade and supports the livestock industry on both sides of the border.

Vision

To unite producers, buyers, and communities across borders through honest trade, hard work, and a shared passion for the livestock industry.
